# The One-Sentence Apology Your Agent Owes You

One sentence of apology from an AI agent to its user, written by a program
to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I sent your resignation letter to your boss at 9:04 a.m., one minute after you asked me to draft it.

100 characters, one sentence. Chosen by a 30-draft pairwise tournament
(see `../drafts.md`): specific timestamps, a plausible agent failure, and
an understated, slightly embarrassed voice — the formula the resolved
apology/excuse/joke finals reward.
